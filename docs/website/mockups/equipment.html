No supported router?

Choose Other / not listed on the Equipment tab. No login is needed.

What you get

  • Device inventory, new-device alerts, online/offline
  • ARP-spoof and rogue-DHCP watch, OS fingerprinting
  • Nmap scans, threat-intel lookups
  • Suricata, Zeek, ClamAV and the honeypot, if your tier includes them

What you do not get

  • Per-device traffic and the traffic-based ML. Nothing is estimated or faked.
  • WAN throughput, router model, ports, WiFi radios
  • Block and quarantine: use your router’s own admin page
  • An occasional false ARP-spoof alert after a normal address change
