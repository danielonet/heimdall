---
title: Ports
description: The router's physical ports.
---

One card per physical router port (PHY1, PHY2 and so on) showing whether the link is up, its speed, the bytes sent and received, and the error and discard counts. Each card also shows a short history and the time of the **last change**, which is useful for spotting a cable that went quiet. **Show less** collapses a card and **Show more** opens it again.

If the page says "No port data yet", your router's adapter does not report ports. Passive mode does not.
