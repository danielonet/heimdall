---
title: Network map
description: A picture of your network by urgency and access point.
---

Heimdall sits at the centre. Every device is a dot around it.

- **Distance from the centre is urgency.** The rings are labelled *Threat* (closest), *Watch* and *Calm*. A device with serious open alerts sits nearer the middle.
- **Angle is the network segment.** Each device is placed in the direction of the access point or segment it uses, for example your main WiFi, a guest network, **Wired**, or **Not associated** for a device that is not connected to any of them.
- **Colour** shows state: red for critical or blocked, yellow for a warning or quarantined, amber for online, grey for offline. The last legend entry marks WiFi devices that have been overheard nearby but never joined your network (see [WiFi scan](/manual/wifi-scan/)).
- A device with a critical alert pulses.
- **Click a dot** to open that device. A nearby device that never joined opens the WiFi page instead.

The map counts devices in its header, for example "12 devices".
